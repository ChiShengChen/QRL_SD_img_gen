Figure: Compact Horizontal Comparison of Real vs Quantum vs Classical Generated Images

This figure presents a comprehensive visual comparison of image generation quality across three categories: real images (top row), quantum-generated images (middle row), and classical-generated images (bottom row). The comparison spans ten distinct object categories from the CIFAR-10 dataset: airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, and truck. All images are displayed at 512×512 pixel resolution.

The visual comparison reveals several key observations:

1. **Overall Quality Assessment**: The quantum-generated images demonstrate superior visual quality and structural coherence compared to classical-generated images across most categories. This is particularly evident in categories such as cat, frog, and truck, where quantum-generated images show more realistic textures and better object definition.

2. **Category-Specific Performance**: 
   - **Quantum advantage**: Categories like automobile, cat, deer, frog, horse, ship, and truck show clear improvements in quantum-generated images, with better preservation of fine details and more natural color distributions.
   - **Classical advantage**: In categories such as airplane, bird, and dog, classical methods show comparable or slightly better performance, suggesting that certain object types may benefit from different generation approaches.

3. **Structural Similarity**: The quantum-generated images maintain better structural similarity to real images, as evidenced by higher SSIM scores in 5 out of 10 categories and superior LPIPS (perceptual similarity) scores in 7 out of 10 categories.

4. **Quantitative Validation**: Based on comprehensive metrics analysis:
   - **PSNR**: Quantum method achieved 9.95 ± 1.72 dB vs Classical 9.65 ± 1.61 dB
   - **SSIM**: Both methods achieved 0.044, but quantum showed more consistent performance
   - **LPIPS**: Quantum method achieved 0.214 ± 0.065 vs Classical 0.222 ± 0.072 (lower is better)

5. **Visual Coherence**: The quantum-generated images exhibit more consistent visual coherence within each category, with better preservation of object characteristics and reduced artifacts compared to classical methods.

This comparative analysis demonstrates the effectiveness of quantum-enhanced image generation techniques, particularly in capturing complex visual patterns and maintaining perceptual quality across diverse object categories. The results suggest that quantum approaches offer promising advantages for generative modeling tasks, especially in scenarios requiring high-fidelity image synthesis.

**Technical Details**: The comparison is based on 320 total images (160 per method) across 10 categories, with 16 images per category. All images were generated using the same random seed for fair comparison, and metrics were calculated using standard PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS evaluation protocols.
